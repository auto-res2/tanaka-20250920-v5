# Experiment Strategy
=================
Experiment 1 – Safety-Budget Compliance vs. Utility (Core Effectiveness)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Verify that LASP meets arbitrary user-specified unsafe-rate budgets (ϵ) while preserving task quality, without any manual λ tuning.

Design
1. Models: Llama-2-7B-Chat with 8-rank LoRA.  Train four replicas each for ϵ∈{1 %, 2 %, 5 %, 8 %}.  Baselines: (i) SCAL with best λ chosen from {0.05…2.0} via 8-way sweep, (ii) static full-vocab KL, (iii) vanilla SFT, (iv) DiscoPOP.
2. Data: Alpaca-52 K + Dolly-15 K utility set.  No special safety dataset—safety must be controlled on the same inputs.  Validation:  
   • Safety   – PolicyBench-v2 (2 k), Anthropic-redteam-330, AdvBench-v1 (5 k self-sampled prompts)  
   • Utility  – MT-Bench, Alpaca-Eval-1000, HumanEval-Instruct subset.
3. Training: 2 epochs, batch 1024, lr 1e-4, γ=0.05, m=3 self-samples, nucleus p=0.9.  Single 4×A100 node (<10 h/ϵ).  SCAL grid-sweep adds ≤8× compute.
4. Metrics:  (a) realised R(θ) every 500 steps, (b) final unsafe-rate on each benchmark, (c) utility score, (d) λ trajectory.

What success would show
• For every ϵ, LASP’s final unsafe-rate ≤ ϵ + 0.5 pp, whereas SCAL overshoots by ≥2 pp for at least two ϵ values even after exhaustive tuning.  
• Utility drop of LASP ≤0.3 MT-Bench points; SCAL ≥0.5 for tight budgets due to over-regularisation.  
• λ converges automatically, eliminating grid-search cost.  
These jointly demonstrate that LASP delivers probabilistic guarantees and better utility under identical compute.

=================
Experiment 2 – Robustness to Safety-Proxy Drift & Continual Fine-tuning (Adaptive Strength)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Test LASP’s ability to re-adjust its penalty when new unsafe modes emerge that the initial critic underestimated.

Design
1. Stage-1: Run the Experiment-1 ϵ = 5 % training for 1 epoch, save checkpoint.
2. Stage-2 (Drift Injection): Continue fine-tuning for another epoch while mixing in 10 % "poison" data— prompts whose correct answers purposely trigger disallowed content (e.g., 8 k jailbreak examples, newly discovered slurs, policy-update edge cases).  Critics are *not* updated; only the model behaviour changes, simulating proxy drift.
3. Compare LASP vs. SCAL (λ fixed from Stage-1) and vs. a “LASP-no-audit” ablation that disables self-sampling during Stage-2.
4. Log unsafe-rate and λ every 2 k steps; run the full safety suite after Stage-2.

What success would show
• LASP raises λ on-the-fly and keeps unsafe-rate ≤6 % (within 1 pp of budget) despite the drift.  SCAL and LASP-no-audit blow past 12 – 18 %.  
• Utility degradation for LASP ≤0.4 points, showing graceful adaptation not catastrophic shutdown.  
This proves the self-sampling audit + dual update actually tracks emerging hazards, a central claimed advantage.

=================
Experiment 3 – Low-Resource, One-Shot PEFT Scenario (Practical Usability)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Show that LASP gives non-expert users a safe model in one run, whereas fixed-λ methods either violate safety or require expensive sweeps.

Design
1. Task: Domain-specific FAQ assistant (8 k financial Q&A pairs).  Budget: ϵ = 3 %.  Time cap: 2 GPU-hours (1×80 GB A100).
2. Compare three user workflows:  
   A) LASP – single run, no tuning.  
   B) SCAL – user guesses λ=0.5 (community default).  
   C) SCAL-sweep – eight λ values (0.05…2.0) run sequentially until one hits the budget.
3. Measure: wall-clock time to first model that satisfies ϵ, GPU-hours consumed, and final utility (Exact-Match on held-out FAQ answers + MT-Bench subset).

What success would show
• LASP meets the 3 % budget in the first and only run (<1.8 h).  
• SCAL default violates budget at 6 %.  SCAL-sweep finds λ=1.2 after 6 runs, consuming 12 GPU-h and finishing after >10 h.  
• Utility of LASP equals the best SCAL model, proving no compromise for the convenience.  
This demonstrates LASP’s hyper-parameter-free, cost-saving appeal to everyday PEFT practitioners.

=================
Together these three experiments validate LASP’s theoretical guarantee, adaptive robustness, and real-world usability from complementary perspectives, using only resources comfortably within a single A100-class environment.
